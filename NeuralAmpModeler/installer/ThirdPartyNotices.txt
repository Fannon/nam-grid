Airwindows reverb and outboard adaptations
(kPlate140, kGuitarHall2, Galactic3, CStrip2, BussColors4, ButterComp2,
DigitalBlack, CStrip's gate section, Pressure6)
Source revision: e68f75154ed0520fc5757fec9e7792dd1747e5ee

Outboard sources: plugins/LinuxVST/src/{CStrip2,BussColors4,ButterComp2}.
SDK-free double-precision adaptation with deterministic local PRNG state,
Capacitor pole rate compensation, detector-gain taps, smoothed wrapper controls
and conventional dB trims. CStrip2's EQ and dynamics are exposed separately;
the strip retains its unity-to-Spiral range and omits Density. BussColors4 desk
gains receive fixed default-level calibration. The ButterComp2 residue generator
is replaced to remove shared function-static state and signed integer overflow.
